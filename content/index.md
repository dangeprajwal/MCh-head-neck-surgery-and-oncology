# Welcome to Head & Neck Oncology Resource Hub

This site is a curated collection of academic material, clinical notes, and research references focused on **Head & Neck Oncology**.  
It is designed to serve as a resource for residents, fellows, consultants, and researchers in the field.  

---

## 📚 Sections

- **Theory Questions Archive**  
  A categorized database of all MCh Head & Neck Surgery and Oncology theory questions since 2008.  
  [Browse Questions](./questions.md)

- **Research Articles & Critical Appraisals**  
  Summaries and critical analyses of landmark papers and recent publications.  
  [Explore Research](./research.md)

- **Clinical Notes & Protocols**  
  Structured notes on diagnosis, staging, surgical techniques, and treatment protocols.  
  [View Notes](./clinical.md)

- **Academic Projects**  
  Ongoing institutional studies, including AR-based models, ICA knuckle classification, and AI-based predictive models.  
  [See Projects](./projects.md)
